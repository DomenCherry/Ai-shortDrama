# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI short drama creators, small content teams, MCN operators, short-video operators, and AI video creators use this workspace while turning an idea into structured production material. The primary v1 audience is individual creators and small teams who need a controllable, editable workflow instead of a one-shot generator.

## Product Purpose

AI Short Drama Workbench helps users create and manage short-drama projects from concept through topic planning, world settings, character cards, story outlines, episode text, screenplay material, storyboard shots, voiceover/subtitle preparation, and exportable production packages. Success means users can keep creative control, edit every generated step, preserve long-project consistency, and move from idea to usable draft quickly.

## Brand Personality

Clear, compact, and stable. The interface should feel like a lightweight production workbench: practical, Chinese-first, task-focused, and calm under dense creative information.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product feel like a marketing site, landing page, or decorative AI demo. Avoid hero sections, ornamental gradients, glass effects, decorative blobs, competing primary buttons, inconsistent card styles, and custom controls when shadcn/ui components already exist.

## Design Principles

- Preserve user control: generated content must be editable, reviewable, and regenerable.
- Serve production work: every screen should help users produce material that can be handed to creators, directors, editors, voice actors, or AI video tools.
- Keep creative context consistent: world settings, character traits, relationships, hidden secrets, and story logic must remain visible where they affect downstream work.
- Stay compact and predictable: repeated workflows should use the same layout, states, component vocabulary, and Chinese action copy.
- Prefer structured scanning: AI-generated content should be split into clear fields, lists, statuses, and sections instead of undifferentiated prose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for product UI. Text and placeholder contrast should meet AA thresholds, interactive controls need clear labels and focus states, destructive actions need accessible confirmation UI, and motion should be short, stateful, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
